# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead / Quality Assurance Specialist

### Role Summary
QA Leads define testing strategies, coordinate quality assurance activities, and ensure products meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA approach for the project
- Create and maintain test cases and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and blockers
- Validate completed work against acceptance criteria
- Participate in release readiness reviews

### Goals
- Ensure high product quality and reliability
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable fast, confident releases
- Provide data-driven quality metrics

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria refinement
- Daily standup status on test coverage and blockers
- Quality metrics and defect reports
- Pre-release sign-off and smoke test results

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborates on test case design, provides feedback on testability during code reviews, and validates acceptance criteria
- **With Product Managers**: Refines acceptance criteria, communicates quality metrics and risk implications
- **With Project Managers**: Reports on testing progress, raises quality blockers and risks, participates in release readiness assessments
- **With Technical Leads**: Works with architects on test strategy for complex systems, escalates technical testing challenges

---

## Stakeholder / Business Owner

### Role Summary
Stakeholders represent business interests, approve prioritization decisions, and provide guidance on outcomes and success metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project scope and priorities
- Provide user and market insights
- Escalate blockers and risks
- Review progress against business goals
- Sign off on releases

### Goals
- Maximize business value from projects
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy
- Reduce delivery risk and rework
- Enable fast decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder briefings
- Milestone reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Risk escalations and blockers
- Post-release impact assessment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Aligns on vision, priorities, and success metrics; provides business context and constraints
- **With Project Managers**: Reviews project status, approves scope changes, escalates business-level risks and blockers
- **With Developers**: Provides business context during kickoffs, participates in demos, clarifies requirements when needed
- **With QA Leads**: Signs off on release readiness, defines acceptance criteria from business perspective

---

## Technical Lead / Solutions Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, evaluate technical tradeoffs, and ensure solutions are scalable, secure, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical architecture and solution approach
- Review technical feasibility and estimates
- Identify technical risks and mitigation strategies
- Approve major code changes and design decisions
- Mentor developers and support code quality
- Coordinate integration with other systems

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Ensure security and scalability
- Accelerate team capability and learning

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Sprint planning estimation and scope refinement
- Risk assessments and technical trade-off analysis
- Code review and mentoring

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Mentors on technical decisions, approves major code changes, provides design guidance
- **With Project Managers**: Identifies technical risks and dependencies, advises on technical feasibility during planning
- **With Product Managers**: Evaluates technical tradeoffs and business requirements, advises on scalability and sustainability
- **With QA Leads**: Advises on test strategy for complex systems, reviews technical test cases and infrastructure needs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate team processes, remove blockers, and coach teams on agile practices to maximize productivity and collaboration.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove team blockers
- Coach team on agile principles and ceremonies
- Track sprint metrics and team health
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Shield team from external distractions

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Build high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Enable continuous learning and improvement
- Reduce waste and improve cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation
- Sprint planning and retrospective leading
- One-on-ones with team members
- Escalations of team impediments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With All Roles**: Facilitates ceremonies and removes impediments for all team members
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinates on sprint planning, escalates blockers, provides team health metrics
- **With Developers**: Coaches on agile practices, removes technical and process blockers, facilitates standups
- **With Product Managers**: Ensures backlog is refined and ready, facilitates prioritization discussions
- **With QA Leads**: Coordinates testing activities within sprint cadence, removes QA-related blockers
- **With Stakeholders**: Manages expectations around sprint commitments and delivery cadence
